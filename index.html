To find your ring size, measure the inside diameter of a ring that already fits you, or wrap a strip of paper around the finger and measure the length in millimetres. Then match the number in the converter or the chart below. For example, a US size 7 ring is 17.3 mm across the inside and 54.4 mm around, which is EU 54.

How to measure at home

  1. With a ring that fits: lay it on a ruler and measure straight across the inside edge, in millimetres. That is the inner diameter.
  2. With paper or string: wrap it around the base of the finger, mark where it meets, and measure the length in millimetres. That is the inner circumference.
  3. Between two sizes: pick the larger one. Knuckles and warm days make fingers a little bigger.
